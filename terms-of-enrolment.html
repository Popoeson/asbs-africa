1. Application and admission

Submitting an enquiry or application does not guarantee a place on a programme. Admission is confirmed only upon written confirmation from ASBS Africa's admissions team.

2. Fees and payment

Programme fees, payment schedules and scholarship terms are confirmed individually with each applicant during the admissions process, ahead of a place being finalised.

3. Cancellations and withdrawal

Requests to withdraw from a confirmed cohort should be made in writing to admissions as early as possible. Refund eligibility depends on how far in advance notice is given.

4. Cohort changes

ASBS Africa reserves the right to adjust cohort dates, delivery format, or faculty where necessary, and will notify enrolled participants of any material change as early as possible.

5. Code of conduct

Participants are expected to engage respectfully with faculty, peer cohort members and partner institutions throughout the programme.

6. Contact

Questions about enrolment terms can be sent to info@asbsafrica.com.
