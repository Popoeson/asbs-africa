Our commitment

ASBS Africa is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including those relying on assistive technology such as screen readers.

What we've done

  • Clear heading structure and semantic HTML throughout the site
  • Descriptive alt text on images
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background
  • Keyboard-navigable menus and forms

Known limitations

We are continuing to review and improve accessibility across the site. If you encounter a barrier while using it, please let us know.

Feedback

If you have difficulty accessing any part of this website, contact us at admissions@asbsafrica.org and we'll work to address it.
