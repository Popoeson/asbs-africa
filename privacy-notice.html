1. Who we are

This notice explains how ASBS Africa ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit asbsafrica.org, enquire about a programme, or apply for enrolment.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you provide via enquiry or newsletter forms (name, email, phone number)
  • Application and enrolment details submitted during the admissions process
  • Basic usage data collected automatically when you browse the site (pages visited, device type)

3. How we use it

  • To respond to programme enquiries and process applications
  • To send cohort dates, scholarship announcements and course updates to newsletter subscribers
  • To improve the website and understand how it's used

4. How we protect it

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Data is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above.

5. Sharing your data

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared with partner institutions strictly where necessary to deliver a programme you've enrolled in, or with service providers who help us operate the website and admissions process.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting info@asbsafrica.com.

Questions about this notice can be sent to ASBS Africa Campus, 4th Floor, Riverside Court, Lagos, Nigeria, or info@asbsafrica.com.
